Paste what your supplier promises. Five agents check it against plots, deliveries and satellite alerts, the rules decide, and you see the one change that would flip the verdict.

Sample mode: the synthetic world in SQLite, no key, no network. Same agents, same rules, the promise above.

How a run goes

  1. Extractorsplits the promise into parts data can check.
  2. Plannerturns each part into hypotheses to test.
  3. Investigatorreads plots, deliveries and alerts through Zetaris (SQLite in sample mode).
  4. Evaluatortries to break each piece of evidence with its own queries, and sends the plan back when a doubt was never tested.
  5. Writerwords the verdict the rules computed, every finding with its rows.
